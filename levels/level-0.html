Level 0: "The Lobby"

Inside Safe Humming

Level Parameters

  • Survival Class: Class C
  • Entity Appearances: Class A (None / Safe)
  • Environmental Dangers: Class B (Low Risk)
  • Habitability: Class E (Unrecommended)

Description

Level 0, popularly known as "The Lobby", is the first level in the entire Backrooms. Level 0 has the resemblance of an old, abandoned office building with yellow wallpapers and brown carpet flooring.

Entities

No entities have been found within this level.

Environmental

The Lobby is mostly a safe level to traverse. Although in rare cases, an area inside the level can become very high or steep. These are mostly avoidable.

Habitability

Level 0 is not a habitable level. Resources such as food or water can rarely be found here, making staying in one place a risky move.

Entrances & Exits

Entrances

  • No-clipping inside the real world.

Exits

  • Finding an elevator in Level 0 can lead you to Level 1.
  • Finding stairs and going down can lead to Level 1.
  • Finding stairs that go up instead of down can lead to Sub-area 0.1.
